# Kissago AI, Content & Rights Policy — SEED DRAFT

**Version:** 0.9-seed  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

## 1. Why this policy exists
Kissago combines human ideas with artificial intelligence to create stories, images, narration and other media. This policy explains how users should think about AI-generated material, ownership, third-party rights, provenance and responsible use.

This policy supplements the Terms of Service & EULA.

## 2. Human input and AI assistance
Kissago is a creative assistance tool. Users decide what to ask for, what to keep, what to edit and what to publish. AI systems can generate unexpected, inaccurate or imperfect content, so users should review outputs before using or sharing them.

## 3. Your original material
You retain the rights you lawfully hold in original prompts, stories, characters, images, recordings or other content you submit to Kissago.

You must not submit another person’s copyrighted work, confidential information, image, voice, likeness or personal information unless you have a lawful basis and necessary permission.

## 4. AI-generated output
To the extent permitted by law, Kissago does not claim exclusive ownership of an Output merely because it was generated through the Service.

However, users should understand:
- copyright protection for AI-generated material may differ across countries;
- purely AI-generated material may receive limited or no copyright protection in some jurisdictions;
- another user may independently receive similar content;
- AI systems can inadvertently produce elements similar to existing works;
- Kissago cannot guarantee uniqueness or non-infringement.

For commercial publication, branding, licensing or high-value intellectual-property use, users should perform appropriate human review and, where needed, obtain professional advice.

## 5. Character consistency and user-created characters
Where Kissago helps users maintain recurring characters or visual consistency, the user remains responsible for ensuring that the character concept and references do not unlawfully copy protected characters, brands or identifiable real people.

Kissago may use technical character references, embeddings, story bibles or other internal representations to provide consistency within the user’s project. The Privacy Notice should describe whether and how such data is retained.

## 6. Real people, voice and likeness
Do not use Kissago to impersonate, defame or deceptively portray a real person, or to create a misleading synthetic representation that violates privacy, publicity, intellectual-property or other rights.

If the product permits cloning or imitation of voices/faces, a separate consent and safety review is required before release. Do not imply that current Kissago supports such a feature unless verified.

## 7. AI provenance and synthetic-content labelling
Kissago should preserve or add provenance/AI-generation information where required by law, platform rules or responsible product design.

The engineering/legal team must assess the Indian Information Technology Rules applicable to synthetically generated information and determine whether Kissago-generated text, images, audio or video require visible labels, embedded metadata/identifiers or other technical measures.

Where an exported asset is labelled or carries provenance metadata, users must not use Kissago tools to intentionally remove mandatory legal provenance markings.

## 8. Publishing and sharing
Before publishing a generated story or asset, users should check:
- factual claims where accuracy matters;
- names and likenesses of real people;
- copyrighted or trademarked material;
- age-appropriateness;
- private/personal information;
- whether required AI-generated-content disclosures are present.

Publicly shared content may be moderated under the Safety, Community & Grievance Policy.

## 9. Training and product improvement
The production position on training must match actual architecture and vendor agreements.

Recommended default:
- private story content should not be used to train a general-purpose model for unrelated users unless the account holder separately opts in;
- aggregated or de-identified operational metrics may be used to improve reliability and usability where lawful;
- third-party AI provider retention/training terms must be reviewed and accurately reflected in Privacy.

Do not publish a “we never train on your data” claim unless technically and contractually true across every production provider.

## 10. Safety filters
Kissago may use automated classifiers, model safeguards, prompt filtering, blocklists, human review or other safety systems. These systems are imperfect and may sometimes block benign content or fail to detect problematic content.

Users should report unsafe or inappropriate output through [REPORT CHANNEL].

## 11. Takedowns and rights complaints
A person who believes Kissago-hosted public content infringes their copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity or other rights may submit a complaint to [RIGHTS/GRIEVANCE CONTACT].

A valid complaint should identify:
- the complainant and contact details;
- the specific content/URL;
- the right claimed;
- enough information to evaluate the complaint;
- a good-faith statement that the complaint is accurate.

Kissago may remove or restrict content while reviewing a complaint where appropriate.

## 12. No legal guarantee of ownership
Nothing in Kissago’s product UI should promise that a user automatically receives copyright, exclusive rights or infringement clearance in AI-generated Output. Product marketing, FAQs and subscription pages should use wording consistent with this policy.
