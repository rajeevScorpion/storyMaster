**Version:** 0.9-seed

## 1. Our approach
Kissago is intended to support imaginative, age-appropriate and responsible storytelling. We use product controls, safety systems, moderation and user reporting to reduce harmful or unlawful use while preserving creative expression.

## 2. Child safety
Content or behaviour involving child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation, grooming, trafficking or sexualisation of minors is prohibited.

Where Kissago is used by children, the product should favour adult-managed accounts/profiles, age-appropriate defaults and limited public exposure. Direct child accounts require a separate verified-parent/guardian compliance flow.

## 3. Prohibited public/community content
Where public sharing exists, users must not publish content that:
- is unlawful;
- exploits or endangers children;
- contains credible threats or instructions for serious wrongdoing;
- contains non-consensual intimate imagery;
- unlawfully discloses personal information;
- is fraudulent or malicious;
- infringes intellectual-property or personality rights;
- is designed to bypass platform safety controls;
- otherwise violates the Terms.

## 4. AI-generated content
Generated content may occasionally be unsuitable despite safeguards. Users should report concerning output. Kissago may use those reports to investigate safety issues and improve controls consistent with the Privacy Notice.

## 5. Reporting
Provide a visible report mechanism for applicable public content and a support channel for private-generation issues.

Recommended report categories:
- child safety;
- sexual/inappropriate content;
- violence or dangerous content;
- harassment/hate;
- privacy/impersonation;
- copyright/trademark/rights;
- spam/fraud;
- other.

## 6. Moderation actions
Depending on severity and context, Kissago may:
- block a generation request;
- hide/remove public content;
- disable sharing;
- warn a user;
- temporarily restrict features;
- suspend or terminate an account;
- preserve/report information where legally required for serious safety incidents.

Moderation decisions should be proportionate and documented where practical.

## 7. Appeals
Where appropriate, users may request review of an enforcement decision through [APPEAL CHANNEL]. The final implementation should state any legally required grievance/appeal timelines that actually apply to Kissago.

Kissago support is not an emergency service. If a user believes a person is in immediate danger, they should contact appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement services.
