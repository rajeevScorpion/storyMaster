## 1. What this Notice covers
This Notice explains how Kissago collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data when you use our website, applications, AI storytelling tools, subscriptions, support and related services.

Kissago is designed for creative storytelling and may be used by parents, families, educators, creators and children under appropriate adult supervision. Because story creation can involve names, images, voices and personal details, users should avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in prompts or uploads.

## 3. Data we may process
The final production notice should list only categories confirmed by code and vendor configuration.

### A. Account and identity data
Examples may include:
- name or display name;
- email address;
- account identifier;
- authentication-provider identifier;
- profile settings;
- age/age-band or adult-account confirmation, if implemented;
- parent/guardian or educator relationship information, if implemented.

### B. Story and creative content
Depending on the feature, users may provide:
- story prompts and instructions;
- character names and descriptions;
- story beats, story bibles and generated text;
- uploaded images or reference material;
- audio, narration or voice material;
- generated images, audio, video, books or other outputs;
- sharing/publishing choices.

This content can contain personal information if the user includes it. Users should not include another person’s personal information unless they are authorised to do so.

### C. Subscription and transaction data
We may process:
- plan/entitlement status;
- transaction identifiers;
- billing country/tax information where required;
- payment status, cancellation and refund records.

Do not state that Kissago stores full card or bank details unless code confirms it. Where payments are handled by a third-party processor or app store, explain that payment credentials are generally handled by that provider.

### D. Technical and usage data
Depending on actual implementation:
- device/browser/app version;
- session identifiers;
- IP/network information present in security logs;
- feature usage and performance telemetry;
- crash/error diagnostics;
- notification tokens;
- security/fraud signals;
- cookie/local-storage identifiers.

### E. Support and communications
We may process messages, attachments and account information you provide when contacting support, reporting content, making a privacy request or submitting a grievance.

## 4. Why we use data
Subject to applicable law, data may be used to:
- create and authenticate accounts;
- provide story creation, generation, narration, reading, storage, sharing and export functions;
- save and synchronise user content;
- administer subscriptions and entitlements;
- communicate security, billing and service messages;
- operate child/family or educator features where enabled;
- prevent abuse, fraud and security incidents;
- moderate content and enforce platform rules;
- debug, measure reliability and improve product performance;
- comply with legal obligations and valid legal requests;
- handle support, complaints and privacy rights.

Where a purpose requires consent under applicable law, the consent request should be specific and separate from unrelated purposes.

## 5. AI processing
To provide AI-assisted storytelling, User Content and instructions may be sent to AI systems operated by Kissago or third-party providers. The production policy must identify the relevant categories of providers and accurately describe retention/training settings.

### Training position — must be verified
Recommended default:
- private stories and prompts should not be used by Kissago to train a general-purpose model for unrelated users without a separate opt-in;
- where third-party AI providers process User Content, Kissago should use available contractual/configuration controls to limit vendor training/retention where commercially and technically feasible.

Do not publish this as a promise until vendor settings and contracts are verified.

## 6. Children and family use
### Recommended account architecture
Kissago account holders should be adults aged 18 or older. Children may use the Service under a parent, guardian or authorised educator account/profile.

If Kissago directly creates accounts for individuals under 18, the production system and Notice must describe the applicable parent/guardian verification and consent flow and the data processed for that verification.

### Child data principles
Kissago should apply heightened protection to child-related data, including:
- collecting only information reasonably needed for the feature;
- avoiding targeted advertising based on child behaviour;
- avoiding unnecessary tracking/profiling of children;
- restricting public sharing by default where appropriate;
- providing parent/guardian controls where child profiles exist;
- maintaining age-appropriate safety and reporting mechanisms.

The exact obligations vary by jurisdiction. Do not state that a feature is “COPPA compliant”, “DPDP compliant” or “GDPR-K compliant” without a completed legal/technical assessment.

## 7. Sharing with service providers
We may share data with vendors that process information on our behalf for functions such as:
- cloud infrastructure and hosting;
- authentication;
- database/storage/CDN;
- AI generation;
- payments;
- email/notifications;
- analytics and performance monitoring;
- safety/moderation;
- customer support.

The final Notice should either identify key providers or provide a maintained processor/subprocessor list, depending on the product’s compliance strategy.

Service providers should receive only data reasonably necessary for their function and be subject to appropriate contractual/confidentiality protections where required.

## 8. Public and user-directed sharing
If you choose to publish a story, create a public link, share an export or make content visible in a gallery/community, the content and associated profile information selected for sharing may become available to other people.

Kissago should clearly indicate when content will become public. Private content should not become public merely because it was generated.

Once content has been copied, downloaded or independently reshared by another person, Kissago may not be able to remove every external copy.

## 9. Legal disclosures
We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to:
- comply with applicable law, court orders or lawful government requests;
- protect users, children, the public or the Service from serious harm;
- investigate fraud, abuse or security incidents;
- establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
- facilitate a corporate restructuring, merger or acquisition subject to appropriate safeguards and notice where required.

## 10. International processing
Some providers may process data in countries other than the user’s location. Where international transfer restrictions apply, Kissago will use lawful transfer mechanisms and organisational/contractual safeguards appropriate to the jurisdiction.

The implementation team must verify actual hosting regions and vendor locations before finalising this clause.

## 11. Retention
Do not hardcode unverified retention periods.

Recommended retention framework:
- account/profile data: while the account is active and for a reasonable period after deletion where needed for legal/security obligations;
- private creative content: until deleted by the user, account deletion, plan-based retention expiry, or another clearly disclosed retention rule;
- generated media: subject to actual product tier/storage rules;
- transaction/tax records: for the period required by applicable accounting/tax law;
- security logs: limited period appropriate to security and fraud prevention;
- support/grievance records: long enough to resolve and document the matter;
- consent/Terms acceptance records: retained where reasonably necessary to demonstrate the agreement or comply with law.

Implement the actual retention schedule in documentation and backend jobs before inserting exact durations.

## 12. Deletion
Users should be able to request deletion of their account and associated personal data through the product or [PRIVACY/SUPPORT CONTACT].

Deletion may not be immediate for:
- backups that cycle out under a defined schedule;
- transaction/tax records;
- security/fraud records;
- legal claims;
- required policy/consent evidence;
- content that another user independently owns or has lawfully copied/shared.

The UI must accurately explain what is deleted and what may be retained.

- request deletion/erasure;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- obtain grievance redressal or complain to a competent authority;
- nominate another person where such a right applies;
- object to or restrict certain processing where local law provides it.

Requests can be submitted through [PRIVACY REQUEST MECHANISM]. We may need to verify identity before fulfilling a request.

## 14. Cookies and local storage
Kissago may use cookies, browser storage, device storage or similar technologies for:
- sign-in/session continuity;
- security;
- preferences;
- local caching/offline behaviour;
- product analytics where enabled.

Necessary storage should be distinguished from optional analytics/marketing technologies where applicable. If the website targets jurisdictions requiring cookie consent, deploy an appropriate consent mechanism rather than relying only on this Notice.

## 15. Security
Kissago uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the Service and the risks involved. These may include access controls, environment separation, encrypted transport, vendor security controls, logging and backups where implemented.

Do not promise “100% security”, “end-to-end encryption” or a specific certification unless verified.

Users should use a strong password and protect access to their devices and email account.

## 16. Data breaches and incidents
If a personal-data breach occurs, Kissago will investigate, mitigate and provide notices to affected users and authorities where required by applicable law.

Incident-response contacts and procedures should exist internally before this statement is published.

## 17. Changes to this Notice
We may update this Notice as the Service, vendors, laws or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be available in Help & Legal.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before materially changing a data-processing purpose that depends on consent.
