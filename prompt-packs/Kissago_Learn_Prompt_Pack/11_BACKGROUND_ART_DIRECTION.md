# Future Background Art Direction

Custom background images will be generated later and placed into `/assets/backgrounds`.

The route must not depend on them, but the layout should provide purposeful slots.

## Shared visual language

Backgrounds should feel:

- Story-driven
- Calm
- Atmospheric
- Premium
- Spacious
- Slightly magical
- Compatible with dark and light interface surfaces where possible
- Rich in depth but quiet behind text

They should not feel:

- Like generic AI wallpaper
- Like a cyberpunk interface
- Like a gaming splash screen
- Like a children’s cartoon collage
- Like a finance or crypto dashboard
- Overloaded with objects
- Dependent on readable text inside the image

## Color language

- Deep neutral foundation
- Emerald luminous structure
- Ember warmth and narrative energy
- Very restrained secondary hues
- Controlled glow
- Darker or quieter text-safe areas

## Background concepts

### `bg-01-story-seed.webp`

A small luminous story seed or page-like form beginning to unfold in a large calm space. Emerald structure with a faint ember core.

### `bg-02-fragmented-creation.webp`

Disconnected creative fragments suggesting writing, image, sound, timing and motion. They should feel separated, not chaotic.

### `bg-03-calm-attention.webp`

A calm focal frame surrounded by softened visual noise at the edges, communicating attention without overstimulation.

### `bg-04-story-convergence.webp`

Narrative layers—text, image, waveform, frame and movement—converging into one coherent story object.

### `bg-05-character-continuity.webp`

A recurring character silhouette or abstract identity marker connected across several environments while remaining visually consistent.

### `bg-06-episodic-world.webp`

A central character or story node extending into multiple episodes and locations, suggesting a growing story universe.

### `bg-07-create-invitation.webp`

An open, optimistic final composition where a small story seed becomes the first completed frame. Calm, not explosive.

## Recommended production sizes

Create adaptable widescreen masters:

- 2400 × 1350 px or larger
- 16:9
- Keep important content away from extreme edges
- Preserve large text-safe areas
- Supply optional mobile crops later if needed
- Prefer WebP after final export
- Keep layered source files privately; do not include font files in the project

## Integration controls

Each background should allow:

- Desktop source
- Optional mobile source
- Focal position
- Opacity
- Overlay strength
- Blur amount if already supported
- Glow role
- Decorative/meaningful accessibility mode
