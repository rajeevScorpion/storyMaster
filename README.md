# Kissago

> AI-powered branching stories you shape one choice at a time.

🌐 **Try it live at [kissago.cc](https://kissago.cc)**

## What is Kissago?

Kissago turns a single sentence into an interactive story. Tell it the world you want — a haunted lighthouse, a dragon's apprentice, a heist on Mars — and it writes the opening scene, draws it, and hands you the next move.

Each scene (a "beat") ends in a few meaningful choices. Pick one and the story branches: characters react, the setting shifts, and the next beat picks up exactly where you left it — same heroes, same world, new direction. There's no fixed ending and no two playthroughs go the same way.

It's part book, part game, part collaboration with an AI co-author.

## Features

- 🌳 **Branching choices that actually change the story** — three or four real options per beat, each leading somewhere different
- 🎨 **An original illustration for every beat** — your characters and locations stay visually consistent as the story unfolds
- 🔊 **Voice narration in 20+ voices** — let the story read itself to you
- 👥 **Characters that remember themselves** — names, looks, and personalities carry across every beat
- 🧒 **Age-aware storytelling** — pick a tone for little kids, older kids, teens, adults, or all-ages
- 🌍 **Stories in your language** — write and read in the language you think in
- 📚 **A public gallery** — read what others have made, branch off their stories, and publish your own

## How it works

1. **Write a prompt.** One line is enough. "A retired spy gets one last call."
2. **Read the beat.** A short scene, an illustration, and a handful of choices.
3. **Pick a path.** The story moves on, your characters come with you.
4. **Keep going — or share it.** Play to an ending you like, then publish it for others to read and remix.
