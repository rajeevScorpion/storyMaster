# Example Compiled Prompt

This is an illustrative provider-neutral output. A provider adapter may reorder or further compress it.

```text
Create one full-bleed 9:16 vertical image containing exactly four equal panels in a 2×2 grid, read top-left to bottom-right. Use thin near-black dividers only; every quadrant fills edge-to-edge with no outer border, padding or pale gutters.

Visual direction: whimsical medieval storybook illustration with painterly textures, expressive acting and balanced detail. Warm golden sunlight, amber tones and rich red accents. The continuous setting is a lively medieval European market with cobblestones, wooden stalls, bread baskets and active merchants.

Character references and identity:
Master Elrick — elderly scholar with a long white beard, spectacles and scholarly medieval robes.
Leo — curious boy with messy brown hair and a simple medieval tunic.
Use the supplied reference portrait for each named character. Preserve the same face, clothing, proportions, colours and distinguishing features wherever that character appears. Show each named character no more than once per panel.

Panels:
Top-left — Wide establishing shot of the cheerful market square, colourful stalls, bread baskets and animated merchants. Leo and Master Elrick are absent.
Top-right — Medium shot of Leo standing beside Master Elrick in the busy market. Leo looks curious and expectant; Elrick appears calm and attentive. Merchants remain softly visible behind them.
Bottom-left — Medium close-up of Master Elrick smiling, stroking his beard with one hand and tossing one bright red apple with the other. The apple is clearly suspended in mid-air. Leo is absent.
Bottom-right — Medium shot of Master Elrick warmly holding the same red apple in his open palm while Leo studies it with a thoughtful, puzzled expression.

Maintain one continuous world and four distinct sequential moments. Avoid text, captions, speech bubbles, subtitles, logos, watermarks, modern objects, duplicated characters, extra or nested panels, thick borders and empty areas.
```

## Why This Is Shorter

- Style and world are defined once.
- Character identities are defined once.
- Panels refer to stable names.
- UUIDs and personality metadata are removed.
- Layout and negatives are not repeated.
- The apple is defined as one continuous prop.
